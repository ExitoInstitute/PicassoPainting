Common questions

Before you get started.

How do I request an estimate?

Call us or use the contact page to share your location, property type and painting needs. Online form submissions will be available after the receiving service is connected.

What preparation is included?

It depends on the surfaces. Your estimate should describe necessary cleaning, repairs, caulking and priming.

Can you paint occupied homes or business spaces?

Tell us about access, furniture and scheduling needs so the plan can address them before work begins.

Which nearby areas do you serve?

Half Moon Bay and nearby Coastside communities are the starting point. Please confirm your specific address when contacting us.

How many coats of paint are included?

Coats and products should be specified in your individual estimate; they vary by project.
