Our services

Expert care for every surface, inside and out.

From a single room to an exterior or business property, every project begins with understanding the surfaces and agreeing on the work to be done.

Service 1

Painting

Residential and commercial painting, inside and out.

Residential

01 / Interior

Refresh your rooms. Respect your space.

Interior painting covers walls, ceilings, trim and other agreed surfaces. Preparation may include filling cracks and holes, caulking gaps and priming repairs, with care taken to protect furniture and floors.

For your space: Cleaner-looking finishes, refreshed rooms and a planned approach to minimizing disruption.

Request an Interior Estimate

What your estimate can cover

  • Furniture and floor protection
  • Surface cleaning, filling and caulking
  • Stain sealing or spot priming when needed
  • Brush and roller paint application as specified
  • Trim, door and ceiling surfaces as agreed
  • Cleanup, touch-up paint and final review

The number of coats and extent of repairs must be confirmed in the written scope.

02 / Exterior

A beautiful exterior begins below the surface.

Exterior work starts by assessing the existing paint and substrate. The company’s published process describes washing, drying, scraping, sanding, spot priming, caulking and painting with protection for adjacent areas.

For your property: A refreshed appearance and preparation that addresses known surface concerns before the new finish goes on.

Request an Exterior Estimate

What the work may include

  • Washing and allowing adequate drying time
  • Removing loose or peeling paint
  • Sanding and spot priming affected areas
  • Caulking specified joints and gaps
  • Covering nearby surfaces and landscaping
  • Coating application and site cleanup
  • Discussing carpentry repairs when contracted separately

Second coats, repairs and coatings are specified per project, not automatically promised.

Commercial

03 / Commercial

Professional spaces deserve thoughtful care.

Painting for business interiors and exteriors, with surface preparation and finishes discussed in advance. The project plan should consider access, occupied spaces and the property’s needs.

For your business: A defined painting scope and a professional-looking environment suited to its use.

Discuss a Commercial Project

What to discuss first

  • Interior or exterior surfaces
  • Space use and access requirements
  • Repairs and protection needs
  • Product and finish selection
  • Scheduling and work-area coordination
  • Review and cleanup expectations

Special scheduling, coatings and other requirements are subject to confirmation.
